The Voting Flow

Four steps, zero privacy leaks

Register

Your browser generates a unique RSA keypair. Only your public key is sent to the CTF — never your identity.

Blind

Your ballot is mathematically blinded before the CTF signs it. The CTF signs without ever seeing your vote.

Sign

The CTF's blind signature is unblinded and verified locally in your browser before submission.

Count

The Verification Authority counts your vote using only the signature — your identity remains unknown.

Security Properties

What protects your vote

Voter Anonymity

Blind signatures mathematically sever the link between voter identity and ballot content.

Unforgeability

2048-bit RSA keys make it computationally infeasible to forge a valid CTF signature.

Replay Protection

Every ballot includes a unique UUID nonce; the VA rejects any ballot submitted twice.
